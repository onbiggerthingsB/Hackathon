The trebuchet

Armies in the 12th century used a counterweight trebuchet against castle walls. The heavy mass sits on the short arm. When it falls, the long arm throws the stone. Set a counterweight, fire, then try a heavier one and compare the ranges.

1× is the lightest mass in this model. 5× is five times that mass.
